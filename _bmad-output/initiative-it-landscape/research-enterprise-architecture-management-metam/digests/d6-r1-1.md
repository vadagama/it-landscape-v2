## Questions

1. Extensibility mechanisms by tool — custom fields/attributes vs custom entity types vs fully dynamic metamodel vs ontology/URI-based typing — for LeanIX, Ardoq, Essential, Bizzdesign, Alfabet, ServiceNow CMDB.
2. Documented failure modes of metamodel customization: field/type sprawl, "metamodel anarchy", governance overhead (practitioner accounts).
3. Metamodel versioning & migration: deprecation of fields/types, migration of instance data, documented processes.
4. Extensible-metamodel patterns from adjacent domains: CMDB CI class hierarchies, property graphs, identity/catalog (e.g. SCIM core+extension), namespaced attributes, typed relations.
5. "Core + extensions": minimal core metamodel plus packaged verticals (Ardoq "Solutions"/use cases, LeanIX use cases/solutions, ArchiMate viewpoints).
6. Practice guidance on how much to model upfront vs let emerge ("start with a handful of entity types").
